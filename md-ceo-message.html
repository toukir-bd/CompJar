ABOUT US

Message From Managing Director

Md. Ashraful Islam Mamun
Managing Director
Crafted Career Ltd.

Crafting Quality. Building Trust. Shaping the Future.
At Crafted Career, we believe that exceptional leather products begin with exceptional craftsmanship. Bangladesh has a proud heritage of skilled artisans, and our vision is to transform that craftsmanship into leather products that meet the expectations of customers around the world.

Our journey is built on quality, integrity, innovation, and a deep respect for craftsmanship. From carefully selected materials to the finest finishing details, we remain committed to creating products that are elegant, durable, and made to stand the test of time.

We are proud to represent Bangladeshi craftsmanship on a global stage while continuously investing in people, quality, and responsible growth. Our ambition is not simply to manufacture leather goods, but to build a brand that earns lasting trust through every product we create.

With sincere gratitude to our customers, partners, artisans, and team members who continue to be part of this journey.

WHY WITH US

Crafted in Bangladesh. Made for the World.

We create premium leather products that bring together skilled craftsmanship, refined design, and authentic Bangladeshi heritage. Every piece is carefully crafted with attention to detail, using quality
