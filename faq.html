FAQ

Where are your leather products made?

Our leather products are proudly designed and crafted in Bangladesh, combining skilled local craftsmanship with modern production standards.

What type of leather do you use?

We carefully select quality leather based on the requirements of each product, focusing on durability, texture, appearance, and long-term performance.

Are your products handmade?

Our products combine skilled artisan craftsmanship with modern manufacturing processes. Many stages involve careful handwork and detailed finishing.

Can you develop custom leather products?

Yes. We work with brands and businesses to develop custom leather products based on specific designs, materials, dimensions, branding, and production requirements.

Do you offer private-label manufacturing?

Yes. We can support private-label and OEM projects, from product development and sampling to manufacturing and finishing.

Can you develop a product from an existing sample?

Yes. An existing sample, technical drawing, sketch, or reference can be used as the starting point for product development and sampling.

What is your product development process?

Our process generally includes concept development, material selection, pattern development, prototyping, sample refinement, quality approval, and production.

WWhat is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product type, design complexity, materials, and customization requirements. Our team can provide the appropriate MOQ after reviewing your project.
